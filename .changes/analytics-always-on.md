`@yesid/analytics` measures without asking. `createAnalytics(preset, { domain })` (in `./client` and the root export) wraps the official `@plausible-analytics/tracker`, now a dependency: it captures pageviews, including client-side navigation, and returns `trackEvent`, typed to the preset's events. Only pageviews, engagement and the preset's custom events are sent, never custom properties or revenue, including events the tracker raises on its own (class-tagged clicks, `window.plausible`). It sends nothing unless `domain` is set and the page is served from that host or one of its subdomains, and it is a no-op without a browser location. Requests carry only the page path, bounded campaign tags and the referrer's origin; nothing is written to cookies or browser storage. `AnalyticsPreset` is now `{ events }`, and `svelte` is no longer a peer dependency.

Removed without a deprecation period. The owner waived both DEPRECATION.md gates (90 days and the intervening minor) on 2026-10-29 for `AnalyticsConsent`, `./consent` and `./policy`, and on {{waiver-extension-date}} for the rest of the old client: there are no third-party consumers and both apps drop consent in this cycle. `@yesid/ui/feedback` loses `AnalyticsConsent`, `AnalyticsConsentCopy` and `AnalyticsConsentProps`, and `@yesid/ui` no longer depends on `@yesid/analytics`. `@yesid/analytics` loses `./consent` (`createAnalyticsConsentStore`, `probeDurableAnalyticsStorage` and their types), `./policy` (`getAnalyticsPolicy`, `normalizeAnalyticsControls` and their types), `./plausible` (`sendPlausibleEvent`, `PLAUSIBLE_ENDPOINT`, `PLAUSIBLE_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` and their types), `createAnalyticsClient`, `createPathnamePageviewTracker`, `AnalyticsClient` and `AnalyticsClientDependencies` from `./client`, and `AnalyticsStorageKeys` with `AnalyticsPreset`'s `domain` and `storageKeys` from `./config`. Measurement no longer asks, so the consent parts have no replacement. To migrate, delete the consent UI, store, policy and their storage keys, drop `domain` and `storageKeys` from the preset, replace `createAnalyticsClient` and any pageview code with one `createAnalytics(preset, { domain })` call when the app mounts, and allow `connect-src https://plausible.io`.
