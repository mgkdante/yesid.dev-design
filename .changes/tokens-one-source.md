Make the tokens the one source for the Tailwind theme, fonts and density. `@yesid/tokens` adds `theme.css` (the Tailwind `@theme` map plus the semantic color utilities, from the new `@yesid/tokens/generators/theme-css` `generateThemeCss`), `fonts.css` with bundled Inter and JetBrains Mono latin and latin-ext files (OFL), `preloadFont` from `@yesid/tokens/fonts`, new tracking, measure, glass blur, `size.tap-min` and `z.footer` tokens, and a `density.compact` group that `tokens.css` emits as a `[data-density="compact"]` block. `@yesid/ui/base.css` now imports `@yesid/tokens/theme.css`, adds `.glass-chrome`, and `.label-section` reads `--tracking-eyebrow`. To migrate, delete the pasted TOKENS region, any `@theme inline` color map and duplicate `--font-*` variables from the app stylesheet, import `@yesid/tokens/fonts.css` from the root layout, preload with `preload: ({ type, path }) => type !== 'font' || preloadFont(path)` in SvelteKit's `resolve`, and have dense apps such as Transit set `data-density="compact"` on `<html>`. Deprecated in v0.18.0: `generateThemeBlock` and `replaceThemeRegion` from `@yesid/tokens/generators/theme-block` (and the legacy `./src/generators/theme-block.ts` alias); the replacement is `@yesid/tokens/theme.css` or `generateThemeCss`. They keep working, and the earliest removal is the later of 90 days after the v0.18.0 publish date and v0.20.0, since v0.19.0 is the intervening minor.
